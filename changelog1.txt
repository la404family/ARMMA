================================================================================
                        GHOSTS 2035 - CHANGELOG
================================================================================

--- NEW FEATURES & GAMEPLAY ENHANCEMENTS ---

[1] INSERTION & TACTICAL ATMOSPHERE
- Dynamic Light Detection: Night vision now automatically activates with high 
  precision during night insertions, overcast skies, or stormy weather.
- Seamless night vision transition without visual stutter upon taking control 
  of your operator.

[2] MISSION 01: INTEL RECOVERY
- A 3D physical classified document prop now spawns on the ground upon the 
  elimination of the target officer.
- Added an interactive search action with pickup animation: the secret intel 
  is directly placed into the player's inventory to complete the mission.

[3] MISSION 04: CHEMICAL TANKER SLINGLOAD RECOVERY
- Full overhaul of the heavy transport recovery helicopter flight logic:
  * High-altitude tactical approach (65m) above the hot zone.
  * Smooth and realistic vertical descent for safe slingloading.
  * Vertical climb-out ensuring clean clearance from trees and rough terrain.
  * Aerial Close Air Support (CAS): door gunners actively engage and eliminate 
    hostiles on the ground during climb-out and egress.

[4] FINAL EXTRACTION & BOARDING PHASE
- Pre-landing CAS sweep: the extraction helicopter performs an aerial gun run 
  if enemy forces are detected near the LZ.
- Smooth touchdown and stable landing with open side doors.
- Squad & Ally Autonomy: AI teammates, freed hostages, and captured HVTs 
  physically and independently sprint to the helicopter to board.
- Dynamic In-World 3D Marker: a HUD indicator displays the real-time distance 
  and position of the extraction bird ("Board Helicopter").
- Player-paced boarding: the helicopter waits patiently on the ground until all 
  human players are safely inside (no premature or forced teleports).
- Tactical climb & combat egress: the helicopter ascends while continuously 
  moving toward the exfiltration vector, as door gunners lay down suppressing 
  fire on ground pursuers before high-speed cruise acceleration.

[5] LOCALIZATION & MULTILINGUAL SUPPORT
- Full integration and synchronization of all new interactions, tasks, and 
  3D markers across all 13 supported languages.
